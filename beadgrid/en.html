BeadGrid Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026 · Applies to: version 1.0 and later

In one sentence: BeadGrid collects no personal information. The photo you pick and the bead pattern generated from it stay on your device — nothing is uploaded. There is no account, no ad, analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no networking code. Exported PDF / PNG files are saved and shared by you.

1What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login; we do not collect your name, email, phone number or device identifiers.
  • No access to location, contacts, microphone or health data.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no advertising.
  • No third-party analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app contains no networking code; your images and projects never leave the device.

2How your image is processed

  • You import one image from the photo picker or take one photo with the camera. The app only receives the single image you choose; it does not scan or read your photo library.
  • Cropping, pixelation, color reduction, color-code matching and pattern / material-list generation all run locally with Core Graphics and Accelerate. No server is involved.
  • Results (pixel grid, color codes, material list) are stored in the app's own sandbox so you can reopen and continue editing.
BeadGrid does not upload, back up or share your photos or patterns with any third party.

3Where data is stored

  • On device: project name, source image, grid size, per-cell colors and material list are stored in the app sandbox (SwiftData and local files).
  • Exported files: PDF / PNG exports are triggered by you and saved to Files, your photo library, or shared through the system share sheet. Where they go is entirely your choice; the developer cannot access them.
  • There is no cloud sync and no developer server, so nothing is ever synced elsewhere.

4System permissions

  • Camera: requested only when you choose "Take photo" to capture an image for a pattern; not used otherwise.
  • Photo library (add only): requested only when you save an exported image to Photos; your other photos are never read.
  • If you decline either permission, the rest of the app (importing local images, editing, PDF export) still works.

5Children's privacy

  • BeadGrid is not designed for children; its App Store age rating is 4+.
  • We collect no personal information from any user, including children.
  • The app has no accounts, social features, chat or user-generated content.

6Deleting data

  • A single project: long-press it in the project list to delete it, including its edits and export cache.
  • All data on the device: deleting the app removes everything.
  • Exported files: delete them from wherever you saved them (Files, Photos, messaging apps).
  • Because data is never on our servers, there is no "request deletion from the developer" process.

7Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
  • Material changes to data handling will be noted in the app's release notes.
