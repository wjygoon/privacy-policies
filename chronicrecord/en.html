Chronic Condition Blood Pressure & Glucose Log Privacy Policy

Effective: November 13, 2026 · Applies to: version 1.2 and later

In one sentence: this app does not collect or upload your health data. Blood pressure, blood glucose, medication and weight records stay on your device. There is no account system, no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no code that sends data to a developer server.

1What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login; we do not collect your name, phone number, email or device identifiers.
  • No access to location, contacts or microphone, and no HealthKit integration.
  • No cross-app tracking and no advertising.
  • No third-party analytics, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No health data is uploaded to any developer server.

2Where data is stored

  • Local database: blood pressure, blood glucose, medication, weight, symptom notes and measurement times are stored in the app's own sandbox.
  • Widgets: home/lock screen widgets read the same local data through the system App Group (group.com.chronicdisease.healthtracker) for display only; nothing is sent out.
  • Exports: CSV / PDF files are exported by you and saved to Files or shared through the system share sheet. Where they go is your choice; the developer cannot access them.
  • No cloud sync in this version: the Family page, invitation codes and messages are displayed in the UI only; they are not sent to any server and do not appear on other devices.
Deleting the app removes all local records; exported files must be deleted by you.

3Camera and photos

  • Camera / photo permissions are used only to capture or pick a photo of a blood pressure or glucose meter screen, so the reading can be filled in automatically.
  • Recognition runs entirely on device (Vision + Core ML); photos are never uploaded.
  • The original image is not kept after recognition — only the values you confirm are saved.

4Notifications

Measurement reminders use local notifications scheduled by your device. There is no remote push and no device token is sent anywhere.

5Purchases

Premium membership is sold through Apple App Store in-app purchase. Purchases, renewals, refunds and restores are handled by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment details.

6Deleting data

  • A single record: swipe to delete in the list, or delete it from its detail page.
  • All data on the device: deleting the app removes everything, including data read by widgets.
  • Exported files: delete them from wherever you saved them.
  • Because data is never on our servers, there is no "request deletion from the developer" process.

7Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
  • Material changes to data handling will be noted in the app's release notes.

8Contact

Questions about this policy? Email bigwang_develop@outlook.com.
