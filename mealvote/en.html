MealVote Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later

In one sentence: MealVote collects no personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your family members, dishes and votes live on this device, and — if iCloud is on — sync only to your own private iCloud database, which the developer cannot read.

1What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login; we do not collect names, emails, phone numbers or device identifiers
  • No access to location, contacts, health data, microphone or camera — the app requests no sensor permissions
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, no advertising
  • No third-party ad, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No data sent to any server operated by the developer — no such server exists

2Where your data lives

  • Household and members: household name, member names (for example “Mom” or “Xiaoyu”) and who cooks, stored in the app’s local database.
  • Dishes and votes: 60 dishes ship with the app; each round of votes and each weekly menu are stored on this device.
  • iCloud sync (optional): when you are signed in to iCloud, this data is written through Apple’s CloudKit to the private database of your own Apple ID. Only you can access it; the developer has no read access and no backend that could view it.
  • Sharing a menu: only when you tap Share does the app turn the week’s dish names into plain text and hand it to the system share sheet. Where it goes is up to you; the app never uploads or records it.
In other words: your data is either on this phone or in your own iCloud. It never passes through us.

3System capabilities and third parties

iCloud (CloudKit)

Used to sync family data between your own devices. Whether it syncs depends on your iCloud settings; with iCloud off the app remains fully usable and keeps data on this device only.

System share sheet

Exports the week’s menu as plain text to the system share sheet. The app does not know who you sent it to and does not read the shared content.

Beyond these system capabilities, the app uses no third-party services.

4No user-generated content, no social features

  • No chat, comments, photo uploads, public profiles or any form of posting
  • Member names are used only for on-device display and vote rotation; they never appear publicly
  • There is no login and no account system, so there is no account recovery or account deletion flow

5Children’s privacy

  • MealVote is shared by a household, usually operated by a parent; children may take part in voting.
  • We collect no personal information from any user, including children.
  • The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no designs that encourage spending.

6Deleting your data

  • A single member: tap the delete button next to the member on the Family screen; that member’s votes in the current round are removed as well.
  • All local data: deleting the app removes all household, dish, vote and menu data stored on this device.
  • iCloud data: go to Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, select MealVote and delete the data.
  • Because the data is not on our servers, there is no “request deletion from the developer” process.

7Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, this page is updated and the effective date above is revised.
  • Material changes to data handling are noted in the app’s release notes.
  • Previous versions can be reviewed in the public repository’s commit history.
