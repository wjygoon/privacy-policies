ConditionReport Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later

In one sentence: ConditionReport does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. Your property details, photos, damage marks and exported PDFs stay inside the app on your device. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple.

1What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or a password.
  • No device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses or location data.
  • No access to contacts, microphone, health data or motion data.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no advertising.
  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs. The app links only Apple system frameworks.
  • The app contains no networking code and makes no HTTP requests, so your reports cannot be uploaded anywhere.

2Where your data lives

On this device only

  • Property details you enter: property name, address, move-in and move-out dates, report status.
  • Room names, report type, report dates and the tenant and landlord names you type.
  • Photos you take or import, stored as JPEG files in the app's own Documents directory.
  • Damage marks: position on the photo, severity, category and your notes.
  • PDF exports you generate, written to the same app directory until you delete them or the app.
  • Preferences and purchase state, stored with the system's local preferences store.
There is no iCloud or CloudKit sync. If you delete the app, everything it stored is removed with it.

Sharing you start yourself

When you export a PDF, the app hands the file to the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination (Files, Mail, Messages, AirDrop, a printer and so on). We are not involved in that transfer and receive no copy of the file.

3Camera and photo library

  • The camera is used only when you tap "Take photo" to document a room, a fixture or damage.
  • The photo library is read only when you tap "Import from library" to add an existing photo.
  • Photos you capture or import are copied into the app's private sandbox. They are not uploaded, indexed or scanned.
  • You can decline either permission; the rest of the app keeps working.

4In-app purchases

ConditionReport offers a lifetime unlock and optional yearly and monthly subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Purchases, renewals, refunds and restores are handled by Apple under your Apple ID.

  • We never receive or store your payment method, card number or billing address.
  • StoreKit tells the app whether a valid entitlement exists; the unlocked state is kept locally so the app works offline.
  • Apple provides developers only with aggregate sales reports, which do not identify individual customers.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings at any time.

5Analytics, advertising and tracking

  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no advertising.
  • There are no tracking domains and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because nothing is tracked.
  • The privacy manifest shipped inside the app declares no collected data types and no tracking.

6Children's privacy

  • ConditionReport is a practical tool for tenants and landlords, not a children's app; its age rating is 4+.
  • We do not collect personal information from any user, including children.
  • There are no accounts, social features, chat or user-generated content shared with others.

7Deleting your data

  • One report: open it in the Reports tab and delete it; its photos and PDF exports are removed with it.
  • Unsaved drafts: clear them in Settings → Drafts → Clear All Drafts.
  • Everything: deleting the app from your device removes all reports, photos, PDFs and preferences.
  • Because your data never reaches our servers, there is no server-side deletion request to make.

8Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
  • Material changes affecting how data is handled will be noted in the app's release notes.
  • Earlier versions are visible in the commit history of this public repository.
