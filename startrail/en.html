StarTrail Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.0 and later

In one sentence: StarTrail does not collect your personal information. The app requires no account and contains no ads, analytics, or crash reporting. Your habits and check-ins stay on your device — they are written to your own iCloud space only if you turn on iCloud backup in Settings.

1What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or device identifiers.
  • No access to health data, photos, location, contacts, microphone, or camera.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no advertising.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The app contains no HTTP networking code and uses no third-party frameworks.

2Where your data lives

On this device (the app's own sandbox)

  • The habits you create: name, icon, color, frequency, daily goal, reminder time, notes
  • Check-ins and streak counts
  • Focus sessions: pomodoro, stopwatch, and countdown durations and completion state
  • Preferences: appearance, language, sound, haptics, reminders

iCloud (only when you enable it)

  • iCloud backup in Settings is a Pro feature and is off by default.
  • When you enable it and run a backup, the app writes a JSON backup file to your own iCloud Drive using the app's iCloud container (iCloud.com.startrail.app).
  • That file is stored in Apple's iCloud and protected by your Apple ID and Apple's terms. We cannot access, read, or restore it.
  • Turning the switch off does not delete backups already written to iCloud; you can remove them in iCloud Drive.
Apart from the iCloud backup you explicitly enable, the app sends no data off your device.

3Third-party services and system capabilities

Apple App Store In-App Purchase

The app offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions and a lifetime purchase. Purchases, renewals, refunds, and restores are handled by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment method or billing details; we only see aggregate sales reports from Apple, which contain no personally identifying information.

Apple iCloud

Used only for the backup feature described above, and only after you turn it on.

Notifications

The app uses local notifications only, for the habit reminders you set yourself. It does not register for remote push, does not submit a device token to any server, and therefore needs no network to deliver reminders — and sends their contents nowhere.

Haptics and system sounds

Played locally by the system. No data leaves the device, and no microphone or recording permission is used.

Beyond these system capabilities, the app uses no third-party services.

4Children's privacy

  • StarTrail is not designed for children; its App Store age rating is 4+.
  • We do not collect personal information from any user, including children.
  • There are no accounts, social features, chat, or user-generated content.
  • If a parent allows a child to use the app, the child's habit data still stays on the device and is never obtained by us.

5Deleting your data

  • A single habit: delete it from the habit detail screen; all of its check-ins are deleted with it (the app asks for confirmation first).
  • All local data: deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • iCloud backup: if you enabled it, delete the backup file inside the StarTrail folder in iCloud Drive. Deleting the app does not remove the iCloud copy.
  • Because your data is not on our servers, there is no "request deletion from the developer" process.

6Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
  • Material changes affecting how data is handled will be noted in the app's release notes.
  • Earlier versions are visible in the commit history of this public repository.
