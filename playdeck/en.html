PlayDeck Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Applies to version 1.0.0 and later

In one sentence: PlayDeck does not collect your personal information. The app makes no network requests and requires no account. There are no ads or analytics. Everything you create in the app stays on this device.

1What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or device identifiers.
  • No access to location, contacts, health data, microphone, or camera.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No data sent to any server — the app contains no network request code.

2Where your data lives

  • Card content: all 93 family game cards ship inside the app as bundled data. Nothing is downloaded.
  • Play log: the card, date, and duration of each session are stored in the app's own local database.
  • Memory card photos: you pick a photo yourself through the system photo picker (PhotosPicker). The app receives only that single photo and copies it into its own folder to compose the share image. It does not read other photos and never writes back to your photo library.
  • Share images and print layouts: rendered on device, never through a server.
Your data is not on our servers — there is no server receiving it.

3Third-party services and system capabilities

Apple App Store In-App Purchase

Unlocking the full deck is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Purchases, refunds, and restores are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment method, billing address, or Apple ID details.

System photo picker

Used only so you can choose a photo. It runs out of process; the app can only access the single item you explicitly select.

System share sheet and printing

You decide where a share image or printed card goes — to your photo library, to another person, or to a printer. The app does not upload anything on your behalf and does not log your sharing activity.

Beyond these system capabilities, the app uses no third-party services.

4Children's privacy

  • PlayDeck is designed for parents and children aged 3–8 to use together, with a parent operating the app.
  • We do not collect personal information from any user, including children.
  • There are no accounts, chat, user-generated content, social features, or ads.
  • Unlocking paid content requires a parent to complete the purchase through the App Store. The app contains no design that encourages children to spend money.

5Deleting your data

  • Memory cards: long-press a card in the Memory tab to delete it. This removes both the local photo file and its record. Deletion cannot be undone.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes all locally stored records and photo files.
  • Because your data is not on our servers, there is no "request deletion from the developer" process — we never had your data.

6Changes to this policy

  • If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
  • Material changes affecting how data is handled will be noted in the app's release notes.
  • Earlier versions are visible in the commit history of this public repository.
